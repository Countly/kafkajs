## Segmented evidence

Close-and-reopen evidence uses the versioned format `segmented-1`. One observer belongs to one public-client lifecycle segment. A stable socket factory routes every new socket to the selected segment, and sockets already open stay with their original observer. At most two segments per kind are undischarged at once, including one waiting for decode and disposal; a segment is discharged only after every client socket it owns closed, and the fixture fails rather than queueing when the previous segment has not drained at the next boundary. Every observer keeps its 64 allocated sockets, 4096 records and 2 MiB retained bytes, and every live broker sink keeps its 2 MiB bound: nothing is cleared, truncated or reset to fit.

The segment catalogue is fixed by the schedule before any result is read: 21 lifecycles for each of the four kinds, one segment for each of the four zero-timeout arms and one for the blackhole arm, 89 in all. A later soak case uses 7 lifecycles per kind with an upper bound of 16. Each sealed segment is exactly two files: its complete transcript and cleanup JSON, and a payload/proof JSONL bundle. The bundle holds every captured Push payload under its segment-qualified request identity, including empty payloads, and, for each accepted nonempty Push, the raw body, the full independent decode and the decoder's own receipts. Lengths, hashes and catalogues are checked when packing and again, unchanged, when replaying. The archive index lists the segment files of the whole scenario; request keys and socket numbers repeat from one segment to the next and are never global identities. Each accepted Push still joins its successful Get, response, transmitted bytes, decompressed hash, UUID, client ID, subscription, node, terminating flag and one broker sink row, and every sink row is consumed at most once across all segments. The whole archive, shared files included, stays under 512 files and 16 MiB; overflow fails and never omits evidence. A final archive without this format, or without its complete catalogue, cannot satisfy the lane.

The cleanup manifest names every segment index with its digest, format and segment count, and `verifyCleanup` requires one such entry for each stamped segmented scenario.
